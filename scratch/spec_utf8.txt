Project Overview            1.1 Client Information &amp; Project Background                         xpand          Foundation is an Australian-registered charity enabling community-led social and economic development through regeneration and innovation. For more than a decade,          xpand          has worked alongside communities in Timor-Leste to support fair, regenerative, and climate-resilient livelihoods. Our approach is grounded in long-term partnerships, knowledge exchange, and practical innovation, with a strong belief that the most effective and enduring change occurs when communities lead and local knowledge is matched with the right tools, systems, and networks.                                       xpand          operates as a small core team based in Melbourne, working closely with a trusted network of more than 100 Timorese colleagues across Timor-Leste. Together, we bring expertise spanning GIS and spatial analysis, carbon accounting, project and program management, botany, environmental and forest science, and community development. This distributed, collaborative model allows          xpand          to combine technical rigour with deep local insight and on-the-ground experience.                                       xpand          FoundationΓÇÖs work in Timor-Leste is delivered through a set of closely linked initiatives, including           WithOneSeed          ,          Rai Matak         , and          TreeO2         .          WithOneSeed          and Rai Matak focus on community-led forestry, sustainable livelihoods, and certified carbon farming, supporting smallholder farmers to restore landscapes while improving household incomes and long-term resilience.                                      One of          xpand          FoundationΓÇÖs key technical innovations is TreeO2, a digital platform and mobile app developed and iteratively refined over several years in Timor-Leste. TreeO2 tracks trees planted by smallholder farmers, monitors growth over time, and supports farmer participation in reforestation and carbon markets. The platform underpins forest monitoring, carbon accounting, certification processes, and transparent reporting across          xpandΓÇÖs          programs.                                      TreeO2 has now reached a stage where a full technical rebuild is required to support scale, reliability, and future integration. This project invites students to work with          xpand          Foundation on the next phase of TreeO2, contributing to a real-world social and environmental impact initiative. Students will gain hands-on experience supporting the rebuild through AWS infrastructure, RESTful API design, database schema design, and cloud deployment, while contributing directly to community-led climate and livelihood outcomes in Timor-Leste.                          1.2 TreeO2 Application Features                                                       Feature                               Description                                                 NFC Tag Scanning                                       Inspectors scan farmer cards and NFC fobs attached to trees                                                Growth Tracking                                      Height, diameter, circumference, species, planting date, photos                                                Geolocation                                      GPS coordinates and project/location information                                                Role-Based System                                      Farmer, Inspector, Manager, Admin                                                Multilingual Support                                      Tree types, labels, and messages in multiple languages              Farm Management           Create and manage farms linked to farmers and projects; each tree scan belongs to a farm             Farm Boundary Upload           Upload and validate      GeoPackage      farm boundaries and optional eligible planting boundaries                                     1.3. Authentication and Token Model           4.1 Token Types           4.1.1 Identity Access Token                 The identity access token authenticates the user but does not grant project-scoped operational access. Identity JWTs are used only for authentication, project selection, and listing accessible organisations/projects. Identity JWTs must not be accepted for project-scoped operational endpoints.                Rules:                    JWT                    Short-lived, recommended 15 minutes                    Contains user identity and system-level claims only                    Used for login continuation, project selection, and non-project identity actions                    Must not be used for project-scoped data operations                    The identity JWT may include all organisation memberships and organisation roles for context.                        Example Identity JWT payload:                       {                       "sub": 1,                       "         userId         ": 1,                       "         systemRole         ": "         SystemAdmin         ",                "organisations": [                 {                 "         organisationId         ": 10,                 "         organisationRole         ": "         OrganisationAdmin         "                 },                 {                 "         organisationId         ": 12,                 "         organisationRole         ": "Member"                        }],                       "scope": "identity",                       "         jti         ": "         uuid         ",                       "         iat         ": 1710000000,                       "exp": 1710000900                   }              4.1.2 Project-Scoped Access Token                 The project-scoped token is issued when a user selects a project.                Rules:                    JWT                    Short-lived, recommended 15 minutes                    Contains selected project context                    Contains assigned project roles                    Required for most project-specific endpoints                    Must not authorize access to any other project                    The project-scoped JWT must include only the organisation and organisation role relevant to the selected project.                  4.1.3 Refresh Token                 Refresh tokens support persistent sessions.                Rules:                    Stored server-side as a secure hash only                    Device-scoped using            device_id                     Rotated on use                    Never stored in the            users          table                    Refresh tokens must be revoked when account access materially changes, including account deactivation, password reset, security incident, logout, user removal from an organisation, user removal from a project, or removal of all project roles for a project.                    Minor role changes do not necessarily require immediate refresh token revocation because project JWTs are short-lived and permissions are re-evaluated when a new project token is issued.                      4.1.4 Token Expiry Policy                        Token expiry is determined by token type rather than user role. Identity JWTs and project-scoped JWTs are short-lived and use the same expiry across all roles. Refresh tokens are session/device-scoped and use a consistent expiry regardless of role.                                      Extending token lifetimes based on roles (e.g. Inspector vs Manager) is not supported. Users may hold multiple roles simultaneously, and permissions are additive.                                      Offline field operation is supported through cached project data and deferred synchronization rather than extended JWT lifetimes.                        Identity and project-scoped JWTs should use a short-lived expiry (e.g. 15 minutes), while refresh tokens may use a longer expiry (e.g. 14 days) and are device-scoped.                            5. Final Notes                 This specification formalizes TreeO2 as a secure, multi-tenant, capability-driven platform. The key architectural decisions are:                    Users can hold multiple simultaneous roles.                    Project access requires          SystemAdmin          authority,          OrganisationAdmin          authority for a linked organisation, or standard organisation access plus project role assignment.                    Permissions are additive and          capability-driven         .                    Project-scoped JWTs carry assigned project roles. Effective permissions are derived server-side.                    Refresh tokens provide secure session persistence and are revoked when access changes.      Farms provide the ownership and spatial context for trees; every tree scan references a valid farm.                    Tree scan history must be preserved and audited.      Farm ownership and boundary changes must preserve traceability and audit history.                    Reports are generated asynchronously and stored externally.                        This design is intended to provide a scalable foundation for TreeO2 API / DB while preserving operational flexibility for real-world field workflows.
